## First comment (the maker's note)

Hey Product Hunt 👋

Quick framing, because this is not trying to beat YouTube or Adobe at auto-captions. Those are fine for common languages. SubCaptions is for the two cases they can't handle: when you need real control over the wording and timing, and when you work in a language they don't auto-caption at all. I built it partly because I work in a language Adobe doesn't support, so automation was never an option for me; a person had to type the subtitles, and the existing tools for that were painful.

Desktop tools like Aegisub and Subtitle Edit are powerful but heavy, and you're scrubbing a waveform in a window that isn't the video. YouTube's built-in editor is clunky. So I built a captioning editor that puts the captions on the video.

- Paste a YouTube link and it loads right there.
- Play it. Press S when a line starts, E when it ends, type the caption.
- It renders on the video the instant you save it, so the timing is never a guess.
- Export a clean .srt or .vtt that drops into YouTube, Premiere, DaVinci, or CapCut.

A couple of things I made sure of:
- It stays in your browser. Captions are saved locally, nothing is uploaded, and you can close the tab and come back to them.
- You type, you don't fight a transcript. No auto-caption errors to clean up. The keyboard shortcuts make it fast enough to caption a short in a few minutes.

It also doubles as a cheap handoff: if you don't speak the target language, send the link to a translator and they type the subtitles on top of the video themselves, with no software to install or captioning vendor to hire. And once captions exist, there's a one-click translate into 50+ languages, which you can edit before exporting.

Honest about the boundaries: it doesn't auto-transcribe (that's the point, you stay in control), and translation is commodity tech underneath. The genuinely useful part is the caption-on-the-video workflow for the jobs auto-captions can't do. Captioning and export are free; the paid tier is for translating at volume and full-length videos.

Would love feedback, especially from anyone who subtitles in a language the big tools ignore. What does your current process cost you?

## Launch-day timing & tactics
- Launch 12:01 AM PT (Product Hunt's day resets on Pacific time).
- Tuesday through Thursday are usually less crowded than Monday.
- Line up 10-15 people who will genuinely try it and comment (not just upvote) in the first hours. Comments rank you.
- Reply to every comment within minutes for the first 6 hours.
- Don't ask for upvotes directly (against PH rules). Ask people to try it and share feedback.
