Free SRT translator, no signup, no watermark

Most 'free' subtitle translators put your file behind a signup, a watermark, or a daily wall. This one doesn't. Drop in an .srt, pick a language, and download the result. The file is read in your browser, and only the caption text is sent out for translation.

How it works

  1. Drag your .srt file onto the page (or paste the text).
  2. Pick the language you want to translate into.
  3. Download the translated .srt, no account required.

Frequently asked questions

What's the catch with 'free'?

Files up to 200 lines translate free with no signup. Solo and Team plans add unlimited translations, AI auto-captions, and video upload in the captioner on the home page.

Is my subtitle file uploaded anywhere?

The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded as a file. The caption text is sent to our translation service, which passes it to Anthropic's API to be translated. Our service does not store or log the text.

Is there a watermark or line limit on the output?

There is no watermark. Free files are limited to 200 lines and 2 MB. Longer files need a Solo or Team plan, and the translate page tells you when a file is over the limit.
