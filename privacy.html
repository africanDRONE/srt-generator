Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026. This explains what SubCaptions collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. SubCaptions is a browser-based subtitle and caption editor.

The short version

Captions you write are saved in your browser on your device. If you sign in, your projects are also saved to your account on our servers. Videos you link from sites such as YouTube or Vimeo play from those sites. Videos you send for AI auto-captions, or upload on a paid plan, are handled on our servers as described below. We collect what we need to run accounts and billing (your email and plan), and we use a few outside services for sign-in, payments, hosting, translation, and speech-to-text. We do not sell your data.

What we collect

Account. If you sign in, we store your email address and (from Google sign-in) your display name, plus your plan tier. Sign-in is handled by Supabase. Billing. Payments are handled by Stripe; we receive your subscription status and a customer ID, never your full card number. Projects. If you are signed in, we save each project to our database: its captions, any translations, the video link, and a small thumbnail image. Uploaded videos. On a Solo or Team plan you can upload a video file of up to 2 GB. It is stored in Cloudflare R2 storage and deleted automatically after 7 days, or sooner if you delete it. Basic technical data. Like most sites, our hosts may log standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data and we do not use third-party advertising cookies. When you paste a link, the video plays in your browser from the site that hosts it, and we do not copy it unless you ask for AI auto-captions.

How your caption content is handled

Subtitle files. A subtitle file you open on the translate page is read in your browser and is not uploaded as a file. Translation. When you use translation, the caption text is sent to our translation service, which passes it to Anthropic's API to produce the translation. Our service does not store or log the text, and Anthropic's own terms apply to its handling of that data. AI auto-captions (Solo and Team). Your uploaded file or video link is sent to our transcription server, which downloads the video temporarily, extracts the audio, and sends the audio to a third-party speech-to-text provider to generate the text. The temporary files are deleted when processing ends, and the provider's own terms apply to the audio it receives. Share links (Team). If you create a share link for a translator, anyone who has the link can open the shared project and, by default, edit its captions without signing in. Each link stops working 30 days after you create it, and you can revoke a link sooner from the share window.

Services we rely on

We rely on a small set of third-party providers to run the service: Cloudflare (hosting, content delivery, and upload storage), Render (application servers), Supabase (sign-in and the project database), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI translation), a speech-to-text provider (auto-captions), Google (Google sign-in and Google Fonts), esm.sh (delivers the sign-in code library), and Frame.io if you connect your account to load video from it. Videos from YouTube and Vimeo load those services' own embedded players. Each receives the data needed for its function, under its own privacy terms.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to save your captions on your device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Retention and your choices

We keep account and billing data while your account is active. Projects stay in your account until you delete them: removing a project from your recent projects list in the editor also removes its cloud copy. Uploaded videos expire after 7 days and can be deleted sooner from the editor. Captions saved in your browser stay there until you delete the project or clear your browser's site data. To access, correct, or delete your account data, email us and we will action it.

Children

SubCaptions is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we update this policy we will post the new version here with a revised date.

Questions about this? Email support@subcaptions.com.
