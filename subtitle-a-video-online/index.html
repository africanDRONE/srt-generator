Subtitle a video online, free

Subtitle a video without installing anything. Paste a YouTube link, caption it here in time with the video, and export. It all runs in your browser, and your work stays on your device.

Caption a video →🔒 Free · no signup · saved in your browser

How it works

  1. Open the captioner and paste your YouTube URL.
  2. Play, mark in and out, and type your captions.
  3. Preview them on the video as you go.
  4. Export .srt or .vtt, or translate into another language.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to upload my video?

No. You point it at a YouTube link; the video plays from YouTube and your captions are built and stored locally in your browser.

Can I subtitle a video that isn't mine?

You can caption any YouTube video for things like language practice or fan subs. Respect the original creator's rights when you publish or share the result.

Is there a time limit on the video?

Short and medium videos are free. Very long videos are a Pro feature.
