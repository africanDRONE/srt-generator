Subtitle a video online, free

Subtitle a video without installing anything. Paste a YouTube link, caption it here in time with the video, and export. Captioning runs in your browser, and your work is saved on your device.

Caption a video →🔒 Free · no signup · saved in your browser

How it works

  1. Open the captioner and paste your YouTube URL.
  2. Play, mark in and out, and type your captions.
  3. Preview them on the video as you go.
  4. Export .srt or .vtt, or translate into another language.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to upload my video?

No. When you paste a link, the video plays from YouTube, Vimeo, or wherever it is hosted. Your captions are built in your browser and saved on your device, and in your account if you sign in. Uploading a video file is optional and needs a Solo or Team plan; uploaded files are stored on our cloud storage and deleted after 7 days.

Can I subtitle a video that isn't mine?

You can caption any YouTube video for things like language practice or fan subs. Respect the original creator's rights when you publish or share the result.

Is there a time limit on the video?

Videos up to 45 minutes are free. Solo and Team plans remove the limit.
